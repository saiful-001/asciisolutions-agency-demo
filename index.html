FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Still unsure? Message us, we reply fast.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 7–10 days, a full business website 3–5 weeks and an e-commerce store 4–8 weeks. We give an exact timeline in your written plan before starting.

How does payment work?

We usually work with 50% advance and 50% on delivery (larger projects use milestones). We accept bank transfer, bKash and Nagad.

Will I be able to edit my website myself?

Yes. Our websites come with an admin panel so you can change text, images, prices and sections without touching code.

Do you also run Facebook and Google Ads?

Yes. We set up tracking first, then launch and optimise campaigns. Ad spend is paid directly by you to Meta or Google; our fee is separate.

Do I own the website and the accounts?

Always. The domain, hosting, ad accounts and source code are in your name. No lock-in.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every package includes free support for fixes and questions. After that you can choose a small monthly maintenance plan or pay per request.

Can you work in Bangla?

Yes. We communicate in Bangla or English, and we can write your website and ad copy in either language.
